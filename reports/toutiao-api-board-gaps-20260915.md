## ICU data version closure and explicit Lynx routes

The experimental Lynx crash was traced before any wider routing policy was retained. Fault logs `cppcrash-18822-1789528571578` and `cppcrash-20448-1789529261916` both show a null indirect call from `libtttext_lite.so+0x1beb4` in `ttoffice::tttext::ICUWrapper::ICUWrapper()`. Toutiao scans `/system/usr/icu`; the board exposed `icudt74l.dat`, while the source runtime libraries export ICU 75 symbols and carry locked `i18n/etc/icu/icudt75l.dat`. The library constructed ICU 74 symbol names, received null from `dlsym`, and called that null pointer.

Westlake [PR #5](https://github.com/a2hlab/westlake/pull/5), merge commit `44a2af54a8ec2a6f2d54dfe3c7d7ca6846cf7fb2`, fixes the generic source-app namespace by binding the owned, version-matched ICU data directory over `/system/usr/icu` in the private mount namespace. The helper compiled with `-Wall -Werror`; its SHA-256 was `60b89e1102225280e7a92831d07ebe0fa274323f1225cd7014c8813a8411f04c`.

With that fix, `toutiao-oh-icu-lynx-markdown69` loaded `liblynx_markdown.so` and retained a complete 20-item feed with decoded images and no fatal signal. Its full stderr matched on host and board at SHA-256 `ec561bac4f6533fe26c2cf4765fc429d0b97eb673e8aae7193d4ffa7e16ea39e`; its exact-lock ledger ingested 2,187 events and recorded 333 runtime-registered contracts. `toutiao-oh-icu-lynx-pair70` then mapped `liblynxsecurity.so` and `liblynx_markdown.so` together, retained a complete 13-item feed with decoded images, and had no fatal signal. Its full stderr matched at SHA-256 `168208d2bead2805e3d84e4f1ecdeec69b4b0db29b447ee767274da7931de7bf`.

The isolated `toutiao-oh-icu-lynx71` run mapped `liblynx.so` through the same explicit private Android namespace, eliminating its `_ZNSt6__ndk18ios_baseD2Ev` relocation failure. The settled view tree retained 13 feed items and two decoded images; its SHA-256 was `ffd9f36c28ca14c272a5ebe874f5cec5f7d4634604dfc10398c34fcab252f1fe`, and full stderr matched at SHA-256 `0c8a3909a571ab86fc949de1c49d15be859f668e06beb5284af3338a6904171b`. The ledger ingested 2,386 events and recorded 546 runtime-registered contracts. These runs validate the listed DSOs at startup/feed settle only; they do not establish complete Lynx feature semantics.

## Native-window closure, Animax and WASM

The first explicit Animax run (`toutiao-oh-icu-animax72`) advanced past the libc++ relocation but failed with `ANativeWindow_setBuffersGeometry: symbol not found` in the Android namespace. The child then died with `SIGSEGV` in the ART heap task daemon. The preserved full stderr SHA-256 is `583595ab21c9b8d11f5217b32057d76f4dfab247ec8ce38145d7ca089edaddad`; fault log `cppcrash-29876-1789531765437` SHA-256 is `8dfe5107b8fd79ac3603875046a7b550d0619471c2e3a346b7b073419e095117`. This run was rejected.

Westlake [PR #6](https://github.com/a2hlab/westlake/pull/6), merge commit `34c803314a1f4e3ef4ba3c7c32e12b1ff29a1c55`, adds the generic CPU native-window boundary to the compact source-built `libandroid.so`: width/height/geometry, lock, and unlock/post terminate at OH `libsurface.z.so`, including Android/OH format translation and YV12-to-RGBX conversion. OH symbols remain lazily resolved, so the compact DSO gains no framework-only `DT_NEEDED` entries. The provenance-checked package `source-closure/verify/out/webview109-source-native-window-candidate7` built with `-Wall -Wextra -Werror -Wl,-z,defs`; `libandroid.so` SHA-256 is `d27c05662865fe0b87e7c52ba388795f7dd4f82d5f5f46f11be380aa3c6ac49b`. The packaging check now fails closed unless all five added `ANativeWindow` exports are present.

The repaired isolated run `toutiao-oh-native-window-animax73` mapped `libanimax.so`, the new source `libandroid.so`, and OH `libsurface.z.so`. The same child survived the extended settle with 13 feed items, two decoded images, and no target load failure or fatal signal. Its settled view tree SHA-256 was `fbd5af38ae77acfd83edc387478fc081b985c7605c97004822ec8b53d8c1c0a5`; complete stderr matched on host and board at SHA-256 `59180c2da892cdd4a72c29ed47809f0a89fd52768fd7e0b43804048986fe203c`.

The interaction run `toutiao-oh-native-combined74` mapped Keva, Metasec, Lynx security, Lynx Markdown, Lynx core, and Animax together. It retained a complete 13-item feed with two decoded images and no target load failure or fatal signal. Its settled view tree SHA-256 was `a1f0404fdfcf2d6a90dad943e2a274ec4fd6c0d3a6d37dce2ca1f373ae61ef5d`; complete stderr matched at SHA-256 `89ca941fd96258145bad4da5e657342bcf9b542b260a797c08a79dd93c87a596`. Its exact-lock ledger ingested 2,464 events and recorded 632 runtime-registered contracts.

That combined trace exposed `libwasm.so` as one more reached libc++ namespace candidate. An isolated route did not exercise it, so no claim was made from that run. The combined feature-path run `toutiao-oh-native-combined-wasm76` emitted a successful Android-namespace load for `libwasm.so`, mapped it in the child, and remained healthy through the extended settle with 13 feed items and two decoded images. Its settled view tree SHA-256 was `4c045d07d677e4167be4aee91fecbcd5522becafaed18baa4f4b16ce7542419f`; complete stderr matched at SHA-256 `eb51b8de60b8b3f328a48d14990877fef04eede76696658f05d145b524102ffe`. Its ledger ingested 2,462 events and recorded 631 runtime-registered contracts.

Broad automatic namespace selection remains rejected: the retained policy is the explicit DSO list validated above. The final combined trace has no remaining relocation failure for a routed target. Its other loader failures are bare `libworker.so` and `libnetworkpredictor.so`, neither present in the locked APK, plus `liblynx_v8_bridge.so`, whose required app-owned `libnapi_v8.so` and `libv8_libfull.cr.so` are also absent from the locked APK. Those are payload/feature candidates rather than demonstrated Android API shim defects. A tolerated or unobserved load is still not proof of correct feature semantics.
